# Worked Example — Banking Onboarding Service

Starting point: a regional bank loses 18% of new customers within 6 months. Research shows customers don't understand the account options, feel abandoned after opening, and use only 2 of 8 available services.

## Baseline (3–5 sentence subject)

1. Customer opens account in branch (60 min).
2. Customer receives welcome letter by mail (3–5 days) plus separate card and PIN mailings (5–7 days).
3. Customer is expected to activate online banking independently.
4. No further contact until the annual fee statement.

What hurts: long latency, fragmented touchpoints, low service discovery, no early guidance.

## SCAMPER pass — 13 ideas (one session)

| Operator | Trigger question | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| **S — Substitute** | Which touchpoint could become digital? | Fully digital account opening via video identification — 15 min instead of 60 |
| **S — Substitute** | Which channel could be replaced? | Replace welcome letter with a personal video message from the advisor |
| **C — Combine** | Which separate steps could become one? | Account opening + online banking activation + app setup in a single guided session |
| **C — Combine** | Which services could be bundled? | Checking + savings + credit card as a one-click "starter bundle" |
| **A — Adapt** | Which e-commerce approach could be adapted? | Progress bar like social-network profile completion for onboarding |
| **A — Adapt** | Which hospitality experience? | Concierge contact via chat in the first week |
| **M — Modify** | What if we increase contact frequency? | Automated check-ins at day 3, week 2, month 1, month 3 |
| **M — Modify** | What if we increase personalization? | Dashboard shows only the 3 matching services, not all 8 |
| **P — Put to other use** | Which data could serve the customer as value? | Spending analysis from the first 3 months as a personalized financial check |
| **E — Eliminate** | Which step is superfluous? | Eliminate separate PIN mailing — digital PIN setup in-app |
| **E — Eliminate** | Which form could be dropped? | Replace 4-page paper form with 3-click confirmation using pre-filled data |
| **R — Reverse** | What if the customer configures? | Customer chooses which 3 of 8 services to explore first |
| **R — Reverse** | What if we act proactively? | Bank contacts customer after 14 days: "You're using 2 services — want to explore X?" |

## From ideas to 3 prioritized concepts

After clustering and a 2×2 vote (innovation potential × feasibility):

**Concept 1 — The 15-Minute Onboarding** (S + C + E): Account opening, activation, app setup, and starter bundle in a single digital session. Probe: prototype the guided session for one branch; measure time-to-active.

**Concept 2 — The Onboarding Concierge** (A + M): Personal chat in week one + automated check-ins in months 1–3. Probe: concierge pilot for 20 new customers; track 6-month retention.

**Concept 3 — The Adaptive Dashboard** (M + R + P): Personalized overview that adapts to usage + proactive recommendation after 14 days. Probe: mockup + preference test with 10 customers.

## What good looks like

- One operator at a time; each idea changes the mechanism, not just the label.
- Baseline is concrete before ideas start.
- Concepts are 1–2 sentences with a concrete mechanism and a next probe — not sticky-note labels.
- Portfolio keeps variety: one fast win (Concept 1), one experience-led (Concept 2), one data-led (Concept 3).
