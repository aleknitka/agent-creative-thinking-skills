# When NOT to Use SCAMPER — and What to Use Instead

SCAMPER is for **systematic improvement of an existing baseline**. If your subject is not concrete or your goal is radical invention, another method fits better.

## Quick triage

| Your situation | Better fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| You have no baseline — blank page, radical invention, new business model | **Brainstorming**, **brainwriting**, or **morphological box** | SCAMPER needs something to change; without a subject it produces generic variations |
| Subject is vague ("our customer service," "the product") | **Narrow first**, then return — or try `question-storming` / `challenge-assumptions` | Concrete subject (3–5 sentences) is a precondition, not optional |
| You need to break a dominant frame sideways | `lateral-thinking` | Discontinuity via provocations; SCAMPER stays adjacent |
| You need broad divergence without a subject to stretch | `divergent-thinking` (10+ ideas + lenses → cluster → converge) | Quantity-first divergence on an open problem |
| A recurring technical tradeoff blocks progress ("fix A, break B") | `triz-contradictions` | Treats tradeoff as a contradiction to separate |
| You need many sources of inspiration beyond one baseline | `analogical-thinking` / `forced-connections` / `constraint-driven-ideation` | Imported mechanisms, not just local tweaks |
| Critique and ideation keep interrupting each other | `six-thinking-hats` | Isolates modes; SCAMPER assumes you can hold generation apart from evaluation |
| You have candidates and need to choose or harden them | `premortem`, `options-portfolio`, `concept-blending`, `double-diamond` | Convergence and hardening, not more variation |
| You need a quick structured sweep without facilitation | Run **3–4 selected operators** (selective mode) rather than all 7 | Quality beats completeness |

## Don't confuse

- **SCAMPER vs. brainstorming/brainwriting vs. morphological box:**
  - SCAMPER: medium volume (7–30 targeted variations), medium-high elaboration, incremental innovation, 30–45 min.
  - Brainstorming: high volume (30–70), low elaboration, divergent/open, 15–25 min.
  - Brainwriting: high volume (70–108), egalitarian silent pass, 30 min.
  - Morphological box: systematic combinatorial exploration, high elaboration, 60–120 min, medium learning effort.
- All 7 operators superficially in 30 min is the most common failure — prefer 3–4 deep passes.

## Handoff triggers

When SCAMPER isn't the right entry point, triage with `thinking-router` (*better question / more ideas / broader perspectives / stronger options*).

SCAMPER hands off to:

| Next | Trigger |
|---|---|
| `premortem` | You have 2–3 prioritized concepts and need to stress-test before commitment |
| `options-portfolio` | You have many ideas and need a balanced portfolio, not a single pick |
| `lateral-thinking` | Variations cluster — need a provocation to break the pattern |
| `analogical-thinking` | An Adapt idea points to another domain's mechanism |
| `concept-blending` | Two partial concepts have complementary strengths worth synthesizing |
| `six-thinking-hats` | Evaluation needs deliberate mode separation (e.g., Yellow vs. Black) |

## Anti-patterns

- No concrete starting point defined — ideas remain arbitrary and vague.
- Using SCAMPER for radical innovation — leverage is missing without a baseline.
- No follow-up — clustering, evaluation, and next-step definition are planned before you start, not after.
