# Comparing Picture and CATWOE in Debate

## Purpose

The comparison surfaces worldview clashes before modeling. Show the rich picture and the CATWOE to those whose worldviews differ and record what changes.

## Procedure

1. Present the picture as observed, not interpreted — "here is what we saw" before "here is what we think it means."
2. Present the CATWOE root definition alongside the picture.
3. Ask each stakeholder: where does W not match their worldview? What transformation would they define instead?
4. Record dissent and the alternative W each person would hold.

## Output

One picture plus one CATWOE per debated system, with dissent noted. Dissent is not resolved here — it is the input to stakeholder mapping or SSM conceptual modeling.

## From debate to model

After debate, decide whether to build a causal loop diagram, a stakeholder map, or an SSM conceptual model — each starting from the CATWOE that survived debate.
