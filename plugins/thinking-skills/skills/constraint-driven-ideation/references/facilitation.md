# Facilitation — Timing and Modes

## Timing

- Intake 5 min, one constraint 10 min, second constraint 10 min, third 10 min, transfer 10 min. Total ~45 min for three constraints; ~20 min solo with two.

## Rules

- One constraint at a time; do not hedge by softening two together.
- Independent generation before sharing; no evaluation during generation.
- Solo/AI mode: run timed solo passes per constraint, then self-extract mechanisms before transfer.

## Artifacts

Raw concepts per constraint, extracted mechanisms, 2–3 transferable ideas with probes.
