# Worked Example — Onboarding as Invitation → Excursion → Springboards

Problem: onboarding activation feels like a gate; invited users arrive without intent.

## Four analogies

- **Personal:** "I am the invite — I am forwarded, then ignored at the inbox threshold."
- **Direct:** culinary apprenticeship — a newcomer is paired with a dish, not lectured.
- **Symbolic:** "guided abandonment" — the system guides by abandoning the push.
- **Fantasy:** activation happens ambiently as the artefact is created — no message at all.

Most evocative: **symbolic — guided abandonment.**

## Excursion

Explore guided abandonment as a world: abandonment that is guided — what rules govern it? Recorded as a separate page: the guide creates conditions, then steps back; structure does the work.

## Forced fits and springboards

- Forced fit: in the excursion world, the guide's structure governs by affordance, not instruction → springboard: "An onboarding that guides by artifact affordance, not by tour."
- Forced fit: abandonment is timed to competence signals, not to schedule → springboard: "Suppress push when artifact action is observed."

Probes: one artifact-driven first open suppressing push; signal: activation without push within 24h.

## Selection

Keep two springboards (artifact affordance, timed suppression); both are mechanism-bearing and probe-able.
