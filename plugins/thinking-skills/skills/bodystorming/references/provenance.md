# Provenance — Attribution, Origin, and Evidence

## Named lineage

- **Bodystorming** — enacted, embodied ideation for service and spatial interaction, drawing on HCI and service-design traditions (emergence in 1990s–2000s participatory design and interaction design). A walking-vs-room pilot is captured in `1308.4978` (Wang et al., 2013).

## Adaptations in this skill

- The scene template (before/during/after), role-swap rule, and gap taxonomy (threshold, handoff, waiting, recovery) are practical adaptations for workshop and AI-assisted use.
- Call adaptations adaptations when attributing externally.

## Evidence and limits

- Controlled evaluations of bodystorming as a standalone protocol are limited and context-dependent; the walking-vs-room study reports that walking can be effective but with the caveats of a pilot. This skill is presented as a disciplined heuristic for surfacing spatial and timing gaps, not as an empirically validated intervention.
- Tag evidence quality (observed friction vs. interpretation) and probe results as fact/assumption/speculation.
