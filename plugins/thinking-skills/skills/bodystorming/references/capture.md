# Capturing and Clustering Observations

## What to record

For each friction, note:

- **Moment:** time, location, role.
- **What bodies did:** hesitated, reached, collided, waited, improvised.
- **What the diagram missed:** visibility, sequencing, threshold, recovery.

Keep observation language before interpretation: "P2 waited 40s at threshold" before "threshold is unclear."

## Clustering

Group by mechanism:

- **Threshold:** entering, exiting, or deciding to commit.
- **Handoff:** information, object, or responsibility changing hands.
- **Waiting:** idle time with no signal about remaining time.
- **Recovery:** error, confusion, or exception handling.

Expect 2–4 clusters from a 20-minute enactment; name each as a gap, not a solution.

## From clusters to probes

Each cluster becomes a probe: the smallest spatial or service change, its observable signal, and the owner who can act on it.
