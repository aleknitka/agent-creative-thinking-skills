# Provenance — Attribution, Origin, and Evidence

## Named lineage

- What-if exploration as structured counterfactual variation has roots in scenario methods and counterfactual reasoning traditions; it is adapted here as a single-assumption branching technique for blue-sky and strategic exploration.

## Adaptations in this skill

- The six assumption types (resource, interface, actor, rule, scale, time), the one-at-a-time branching discipline, the world sketch template, and the trace → insight → probe sequence are practical adaptations for AI-assisted use.
- Call adaptations adaptations when attributing externally.

## Evidence and limits

- Counterfactual exploration is well-established as a reasoning scaffold; systematic evaluation of its incremental benefit varies by domain and facilitator. This skill presents it as a disciplined heuristic for bounded world-building, not as a validated intervention.
- Tag evidence quality for insights and probes (fact vs. assumption vs. speculation) and state falsification conditions.
