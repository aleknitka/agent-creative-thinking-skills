# Assumption Types — What to Vary

Use this taxonomy to pick one assumption to mutate. State its current form explicitly before branching.

- **Resource:** what is assumed scarce or costly (time, compute, headcount, budget, attention). Mutate: what if it were abundant or free?
- **Interface:** what channel or surface is assumed necessary (screen, form, meeting, dashboard). Mutate: what if it disappeared?
- **Actor:** who is assumed to act or decide (user, staff, manager, vendor). Mutate: what if it were someone else — peer, automation, external party?
- **Rule:** what policy or norm is assumed fixed (approval, pricing, eligibility). Mutate: what if the rule were lifted or reversed?
- **Scale:** what size or scope is assumed given (batch, volume, frequency, team size). Mutate: what if 10x or 1/10?
- **Time:** what temporal order or window is assumed (before/after, duration, deadline). Mutate: what if before became after, or the window shifted?

Pick one. Keep the rest fixed so the world sketch stays focused.
