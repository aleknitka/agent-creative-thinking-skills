# Consequence Tracing and Transfer Extraction

## Tracing consequences (2-3 steps)

From the mutated assumption, walk forward:

1. **Immediate change:** what shifts first.
2. **Adaptation:** what actors, processes, or resources adjust next.
3. **Newly valuable or broken:** what becomes an advantage or a failure point.

Keep each step to one sentence; keep tracing honest by noting both positive and negative paths.

## Extracting the transferable insight

After tracing, name the insight as a principle with boundary conditions:

```
Principle: when [relation / structure] holds, [mechanism / intervention] produces [outcome].
Boundary: insight holds while [condition]; outside it, [failure or diminished effect].
```

## Designing the probe back

A probe is a small, reversible test that exercises the principle without requiring the whole world to change:

- What you would try, with whom, in what window.
- What result would confirm the insight and what would falsify it.
- Keep the probe to a half-day or one conversation where possible.

Tag the insight and probe with evidence quality: fact vs. assumption vs. speculation.
