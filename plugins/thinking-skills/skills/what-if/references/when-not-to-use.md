# When NOT to Use What-If — and What to Use Instead

## Quick triage

| Your situation | Better fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| You need multiple futures compared systematically | `scenario-planning` | Structured comparison across uncertainties |
| You need ripple effects from a change | `futures-wheel` | First/second/nth-order consequence mapping |
| You need to walk backward from a desired future | `backcasting` | Starts from the future and steps back |
| You have a concrete baseline to improve | `scamper` | Systematic variation of what exists |
| You need breadth on an open problem | `divergent-thinking` | Quantity-first divergence before world-building |
| Facts are missing | **Research first** | World-building on thin ground produces fiction |
| Candidates exist and need hardening | `premortem` | Convergence, not ideation |

## Don't confuse

- **What-if vs. scenario-planning:** what-if varies one assumption and traces; scenario-planning compares multiple futures built from two key uncertainties.
- **What-if vs. futures-wheel:** both trace consequences, but what-if is mutation-led; futures-wheel is change-led with explicit order layers.
- **What-if vs. lateral-thinking:** lateral provokes deliberately wrong statements; what-if mutates one assumption and follows it honestly.

## Handoff triggers

When this skill isn't the right entry point, triage with `thinking-router`.

This skill hands off to:

| Next | Trigger |
|---|---|
| `scenario-planning` | You need to compare several branching worlds side by side |
| `futures-wheel` | You want to trace ripple effects from a chosen mutation |
| `backcasting` | A what-if world looks desirable and you want a path back |
| `lateral-thinking` | The mutation suggests a provocation worth pursuing |
| `premortem` | You want to stress-test before carrying the insight forward |

## Anti-patterns

- Mutating several assumptions at once — the world becomes unfalsifiable.
- Long world narratives before tracing — keep sketches to 3-5 sentences.
- Treating a world sketch as the deliverable rather than the probe back.
