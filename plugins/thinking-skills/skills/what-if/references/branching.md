# Branching — One World at a Time

Change one assumption, keep others fixed, and sketch the alternative world in 3-5 sentences.

## World sketch template

```
Assumption mutated: [type + current form → new form]
World sketch: 3-5 sentences describing how the situation would operate under the mutation.
Kept fixed: [which other assumptions stay as they are — explicitly]
```

## Rules

- One mutation per branch; if you vary two, split into two branches.
- Keep the sketch concise — enough to trace consequences, not a full narrative.
- Name what stays fixed so the branch stays comparable to reality.
- Generate 2-3 branches at most before extracting insight — depth over coverage.

## From sketches to traces

World sketches are the input to consequence tracing. Do not judge a branch until its consequences are walked forward.
