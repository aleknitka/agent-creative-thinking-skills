# Constraint Types and Fact Table

Use this scan to separate what must be true from what is usually done.

## Types

- **Fundamental — physics or invariant:** cannot be violated without changing the world (latency, conservation, information limits, thermodynamic bounds, mathematical invariants).
- **Regulation or hard deadline:** external authority or contract — source it to a document, date, and owner. A "policy says" without a document is inherited until sourced.
- **Observed fact:** directly measured behavior or outcome — source it to data, not to recall.
- **Inherited convention:** benchmark, prior design, analogue, habit, or default — "we've always" and "industry standard" live here.
- **Preference or risk tolerance:** taste, brand, comfort with risk — legitimate but not a constraint until the owner is named.

## Fact table

```
Statement                    | Source         | Type        | Evidence that would move it
Onboarding must be tutorial  | Habit / prior  | Inherited   | One team succeeding without it
Activation must be <7 days   | Metric / goal  | Preference  | Goal change or owner decision
Data residency in EU         | Regulation     | Regulation  | Legal memo, dated
First value needs <1 sec     | Physics / UX   | Fundamental | Measured latency bound
```

Every "must" gets a row. Unsourced is inherited until proven otherwise.
