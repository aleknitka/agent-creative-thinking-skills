# Provenance — Attribution, Origin, and Evidence

## Named lineage

- **Aristotle** — distinction of first principles (archai) as starting points of demonstration.
- **René Descartes** — method of doubt and rebuilding from foundations (*Discourse on Method*, 1637).
- **Elon Musk** — popularizer of "reasoning from first principles" in product and manufacturing contexts (2010s).

## Adaptations in this skill

- The constraint-type scan, stripped-problem template, and rebuild heuristics (minimal, justified-pattern, deliberately strange) are practical adaptations for AI-assisted use.
- Call adaptations adaptations when attributing externally.

## Evidence and limits

- "First-principles" has no single controlled trial base as a standalone protocol; it is presented here as a disciplined heuristic for separating fundamentals from conventions.
- Tag evidence quality (fact / assumption / speculation) and make falsification conditions explicit.

Check permissions before bundling proprietary constraint libraries. The helper `scripts/decompose.py` reproduces no proprietary material.
