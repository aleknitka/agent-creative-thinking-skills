# Laddering — Why-Up and How-Down

## Why-up

Ask "Why does this matter?" and "What is this in service of?" until the answer is a goal or outcome, not a restated solution. Stop when further why adds no new stakeholder or purpose, or becomes universal ("people are happy").

Prompts:

- Why does this matter to the beneficiary?
- What outcome would make this level irrelevant?
- Who else would care about the answer?

## How-down

Ask "How could this happen?" and "What would have to be true?" until the answer is an observable mechanism or condition. Stop at something you can test.

Prompts:

- What mechanism would produce this?
- What condition, if present, would make this happen?
- What is one enabling factor you could probe today?

## When to stop

Up: no new stakeholder or purpose, or you leave the scope you can influence.
Down: the rung is observable and you can name a probe.
