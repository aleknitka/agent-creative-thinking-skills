# Leverage — Choosing the Intervention Level

Score candidate rungs on three axes (1-5 each):

- **Impact:** how much changes if this moves.
- **Controllability:** can you actually act here within your scope.
- **Evidence:** how well supported the link between this rung and the goal is.

Prefer a rung where all three are adequate. If impact is high but control is low, it is strategy, not leverage; if control is high but impact is low, it is busywork.

Design the intervention at the chosen level plus one supporting move above or below it — a single-rung move rarely holds.
