# Provenance — Attribution, Origin, and Evidence

## Named lineage

- **Gregory Bateson** — logical types and levels of abstraction.
- **Means-end chain** and laddering interviews in consumer research (Gutman, Reynolds).
- Coaching and design traditions use why-up / how-down as facilitation moves.

## Adaptations in this skill

- The five-level schema (vision/purpose/function/mechanism/action) and leverage scoring are practical adaptations for AI-assisted use.
- Call adaptations adaptations when attributing externally.

## Evidence and limits

- Laddering is a facilitation heuristic; controlled evaluation of its incremental benefit varies by context. Present as disciplined technique, not validated intervention.
