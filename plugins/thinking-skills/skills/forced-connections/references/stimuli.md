# Stimuli — Types and Picking

Random stimulus forces distance.

## Types

- **Random word:** word bank, seeded picker — distant on purpose.
- **Random object:** physical object properties (material, structure, affordance).
- **Image:** visual scene — list properties, not mood.
- **Concept:** abstract system (ecosystem, market, transit) — map structure.

## Picking

- Use a word bank or seeded picker; avoid choosing something "relevant" — relevance kills distance.
- Seeded picks (scripts/connect.py) give reproducibility; reshuffle to avoid patterning.
- One stimulus at a time; depth on one beats shallow on three.
