# Disney Creative Strategy

Dream big, plan real, criticize usefully — but never at the same time. This method separates three thinking rooms so optimism is not confused with feasibility and critique does not kill exploration prematurely.

The failure mode it prevents is merging vision and judgment in one conversation, which produces timid ideas with premature caveats.

## When to use

- A bold idea needs a path to execution.
- Critique is crowding out exploration or vice versa.
- You need a vision, a practical outline, and testable revisions in sequence.

If unsure this is the right method, start with [thinking-router](../thinking-router/SKILL.md) — or see [references/when-not-to-use.md](references/when-not-to-use.md).

## When NOT to use (brief)

- You need a quick structured sweep without phasing → `scamper`.
- You need to choose among many → `options-portfolio`.
- Facts are missing → research first.

## Process

1. **Intake — reuse before you ask.** State the idea, whose idea it serves, the ambition, and hard limits. Mark fact / assumption / speculation. See [references/phases.md](references/phases.md).

2. **Dreamer — imagine without constraints.** Suspend feasibility; generate the ideal experience, scale, and impact. No realism or critique in this room. See [references/phases.md](references/phases.md) and [scripts/disney.py](scripts/disney.py).

3. **Realist — build the execution outline.** Translate the dream into steps, roles, resources, timeline, and the smallest slice that proves the premise. Keep critique contained.

4. **Critic — examine weaknesses usefully.** List specific risks, costs, and failure routes — each with an observable signal and a fix, not a dismissal. See [references/sequencing.md](references/sequencing.md).

5. **Revise.** Feed critic insights back through dreamer and realist once: which risks change the vision, which change only the plan? Keep one coherent revision, not a pile of compromises.

6. **Synthesize and probe.** State the vision, the outline, the revisions, and one next probe with its kill criterion. See [references/examples.md](references/examples.md).

## Rules

- One room at a time — never mix dreamer, realist, and critic in one turn.
- Keep realism and critique out of the dreamer; keep vision out of the critic until revision.
- Every critique must offer a fix or a signal, not just a doubt.
- Label fact / assumption / speculation.
- Honest provenance: call adaptations adaptations. See [references/provenance.md](references/provenance.md).
