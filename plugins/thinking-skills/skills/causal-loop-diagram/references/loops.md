# Loops — Reinforcing, Balancing, and Delays

## Closing the loops

Follow links until each returns to its start. Label:

- **R (Reinforcing):** even number of – links (often zero) — growth or decay amplifies.
- **B (Balancing):** odd number of – links — pushes toward a target or limit.

Count loops; a diagram with one loop is rarely the real system.

## Delays in loops

A delay in a balancing loop creates oscillation; a delay in a reinforcing loop creates momentum that is hard to stop. Mark delays with || and note where they sit.

## Reinforcing vs. balancing in practice

- R: "More activation → more peer visibility → more activation."
- B: "More reminders → less attention → fewer activations — balancing toward inattention."

Check each loop's polarity by counting – links; do not guess from the story.

## Leverage hint

Delays are where leverage hides: intervening before the delay pays leverage; intervening after it chases the oscillation.
