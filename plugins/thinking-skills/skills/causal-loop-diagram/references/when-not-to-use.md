# When NOT to Use Causal Loop Diagrams — and What to Use Instead

## Quick triage

| Your situation | Better fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| One-time decision, no feedback | `premortem` or `options-portfolio` | Feedback analysis overfits a moment |
| Situation is messy with no shared picture | `rich-picture-catwoe` | CATWOE defines before causality |
| Ripple from one proposed change | `futures-wheel` | Linear rings, not closed loops |
| Plausible futures under uncertainty | `scenario-planning` | Futures are scenario-shaped |
| Need to choose among many | `options-portfolio` | Convergence, not structure |
| Need stakeholder tradeoffs broadly | `stakeholder-perspectives` | Social mapping without loops |
| Near commitment and need to harden | `premortem` | CLD debates structure; hardening is later |

## Don't confuse

- **CLD vs. Futures Wheel:** futures wheel traces consequence rings outward once; a CLD closes loops back. CLD explains persistent behavior (growth, oscillation, decay) that a wheel cannot.
- **CLD vs. Rich Picture/CATWOE:** the rich picture is present-state sensemaking; CLD is causal structure. Draw the picture first, then close the loops.
- **CLD vs. Causal map:** a causal map shows arrows without labeled loops and delays; it cannot reveal leverage. A CLD without R/B and || is not a CLD.

## Handoff triggers

When CLD isn't the right entry point, triage with `thinking-router` (*broader perspectives / better question*).

CLD hands off to:

| Next | Trigger |
|---|---|
| `rich-picture-catwoe` | Situation is messy and worldviews clash before causality |
| `stakeholder-perspectives` | Loops implicate an overlooked group |
| `premortem` | You selected a leverage point and need to harden |

## Anti-patterns

- One variable per arrow without polarity — a map without mechanism.
- No delays marked — leverage hidden and oscillation missed.
- Archetype forced onto the system — let the loops earn the archetype, not the other way around.
