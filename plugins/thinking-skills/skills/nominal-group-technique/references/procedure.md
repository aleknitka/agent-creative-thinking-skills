# Procedure — NGT and Delphi

## NGT (same-room, 45–60 min)

1. Silent write 10 min — 3–5 ideas per person, one per sticky.
2. Round-robin 15 min — read one at a time, scribe captures verbatim on board (one lane per round).
3. Clarify 10 min — author explains each; merge duplicates only with consent.
4. Vote 5 min — each voter scores top 5 as 5-4-3-2-1 on cards; or distribute N/3 dots.

## Delphi (async, 3–7 days, 2–3 rounds)

1. Round 1 — anonymized proposals from experts (no discussion).
2. Facilitator aggregates + strips attribution, circulates summary with median/IQR.
3. Round 2 — experts revise rankings privately knowing the group view; repeat until IQR tightens or time ends.

Virtual board: one lane per person for silent write, one lane for round-robin harvest. Delphi: separate board per round.
