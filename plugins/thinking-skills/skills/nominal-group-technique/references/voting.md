# Voting and Aggregation

- **Ranked 5-4-3-2-1:** each voter assigns 5 to top, 4 to next ... 1 to fifth; sum ranks. Good for 8–15 items.
- **N/3 dots:** if 12 ideas, each voter gets 4 dots across items (at most 1 per item). Fast visual tally.
- **Delphi median/IQR:** circulate median rank + interquartile range per item; next-round movement toward median is expected but dissent retained.
- Tie-break: keep both tied; do not invent a cardinal gap. Record tie rationale.
- Anti-gaming: no negative votes, no bloc pre-coordination, no revote without new information.
