# Provenance — NGT / Delphi

- NGT: Andre Delbecq and Andrew Van de Ven, "A Group Process Model for Problem Identification" (1971); refined in Van de Ven & Delbecq (1974).
- Delphi: Norman Dalkey & Olaf Helmer, RAND Corporation (early 1960s), developed for expert forecasting with anonymous iteration.
- Adaptation: this skill merges both as one ranking pipeline; timings for shared boards; voting aggregation N/3 and median/IQR are standard summaries per literature.
- Evidence: NGT ranking reliability studied vs. interacting groups (Delbecq et al.); Delphi convergence studied for forecasting — strengths are process control, not guaranteed accuracy.
