# Worked Examples — Three Routes

## Example A — Vague brief → better question

**User:** "We need ideas to improve onboarding. The brief is just 'make it better'."

**Intake:** goal = higher completion, constraints = unknown, tried = nothing structured, appetite = medium, time = 30 min, desired artifact = better question.

**Triage:** Better question. Signals: vague brief, prematurely solution-focused → `question-storming` / `challenge-assumptions` / `abstraction-laddering`.

**Scores (illustrative):**

| Method | Trigger | Output | Appetite | Time | Sum |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `question-storming` | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 6 |
| `challenge-assumptions` | 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 5 |
| `abstraction-laddering` | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 4 |

**Recommendation:**

> **Primary: `question-storming`** — generates better questions before answers; you need a revised problem statement first.
> **You will get:** 15-20 questions, clustered, plus one reframed brief. ~15 min.
> **Alternate: `challenge-assumptions`** — if you suspect hidden "musts" (e.g., "onboarding must be a tutorial").
> **Not now: `scamper`** — needs a baseline concept to stretch, which you don't have yet.

**Handoff:** `/thinking-skills:question-storming` (or current equivalent) with intake + note "routed via thinking-router as 'better question'."

## Example B — Have a concept → need more varied ideas

**User:** "We have a draft feature: AI summaries on the dashboard. Need more varied takes before we commit."

**Intake:** problem = dashboard summaries, goal = meaningful user value, constraints = ship in 6 weeks, tried = one draft, appetite = medium, time = 30 min, needs = more ideas.

**Triage:** More ideas.

**Shortlist:** `scamper` (have baseline), `analogical-thinking` (other domains do summarization), `morphological-analysis` (multiple independent choices: source × format × placement × trigger).

**Recommendation:**

> **Primary: `scamper`** — 7 concrete variations on the existing concept. ~20 min.
> **Alternate: `morphological-analysis`** — if you want systematic coverage of the design space.
> **Not now: `brainwriting`** — better when loud voices dominate; use after SCAMPER if you need a second divergence pass.

## Example C — Candidates exist but fragile → stronger options + lateral escape

**User:** "We have 3 rollout plans. Each fixes one thing but breaks another — feels like a tradeoff we can't escape."

**Intake:** candidates = 3 plans, recurring tradeoff, appetite = high, time = 45 min, needs = stronger options.

**Triage:** Stronger options (but may also need a reframe).

**Shortlist:** `triz-contradictions` (recurring tradeoff), `premortem` (before commitment), `lateral-thinking` (stuck on obvious tradeoff line).

**Recommendation (portfolio pair):**

> **Primary: `triz-contradictions`** — names the contradiction and seeks separation/inventive principles rather than a compromise. ~20 min.
> **Then: `premortem`** — imagines each plan has failed; extracts warning signs and hardening changes. ~15 min.
> **Alternate if appetite/time is low:** just `premortem`.
> **Not now: `what-if`** — blue-sky is not the next artifact; you need tradeoff resolution first.

**Handoff:** sequence `triz-contradictions` → `premortem`; pass the contradiction statement forward.

---

## Anti-pattern

Don't reroute a direct request. If the user says "run a premortem on this launch plan," honor it and use the router only to *suggest* what could follow.
