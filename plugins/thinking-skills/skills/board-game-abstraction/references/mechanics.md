# Mechanic Catalog — Pressure → Mechanic → What It Reveals

Choose one — the pressure you pick is the theory of the problem.

| Real pressure | Mechanic | Play pattern | What divergence teaches |
|---|---|---|---|
| Queue / contention / queue-jumping | **Worker placement** | Limited spots, blocking, turn-order fight | Where contention is real vs. imagined |
| Shared scarce budget / capacity | **Resource management** | Spend 2–3 tight resources across actions | Which scarcity drives behavior |
| Repeated small risk / drift | **Push-your-luck** | Draw again or bank — bust vs. keep | Where risk accumulates invisibly |
| Silos vs commons (info hidden) | **Co-op with hidden info** | Private hands, joint win, betrayal temptation | What silos cost when stakes are shared |
| Competence / advantage snowballs | **Deck-building** | Good play adds better cards to future draws | How advantage compounds |
| Threshold / take-off (activation) | **Track race** | Advance on track; stalls cost extra | Where activation truly stalls |
| Negotiation / deal-making | **Trading / auction** | Values differ; deals reveal preference | What each side actually values |
| Event shock / volatility | **Event deck** | Random events hit the board each round | How robust the loop is to shocks |

One mechanic per game. Theme follows mechanic — do not choose "like Monopoly" before choosing contention vs. risk.
