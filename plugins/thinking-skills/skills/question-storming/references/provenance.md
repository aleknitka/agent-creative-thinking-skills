# Provenance — Attribution, Origin, and Evidence

## Named lineage

- **Hal Gregersen** — question burst / question-storming (MIT Sloan, 2018): burst of questions, no answers.
- Related: design thinking problem-framing, liberating structures (1-2-4-All).

## Adaptations in this skill

- Six question categories, clustering rules, leverage/tractability/reframing-power scoring, and probe design are practical adaptations for AI-assisted use.
- Call adaptations adaptations when attributing externally.

## Evidence and limits

- Question-storming is a facilitation heuristic; systematic evaluation varies by context. Present as disciplined technique, not validated intervention.
