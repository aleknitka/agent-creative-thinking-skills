# ICE Scoring

- Impact 1–10, Confidence 1–10, Ease 1–10.
- Two valid formulas — pick one and keep it: (I×C×E)/100 (0–10 scale) or I+C+E (3–30).
- Use only where evidence exists; default dot + impact–effort when data is thin.
- Record ICE beside impact–effort — they are complementary, not competing.
