# SCAMPER

You already have something — stretch it. SCAMPER turns one baseline into 7–30 targeted variations by asking what to change, not what to invent from scratch.

The failure mode it prevents is polishing the same option while cheaper, adjacent variations sit one operator away.

## When to use

- You have a baseline product, service, or process to improve.
- Incremental ideas are the goal — 2×, not 10×.
- Iterations keep circling the same design.

If unsure this is the right method, start with [thinking-router](../thinking-router/SKILL.md) — or see [references/when-not-to-use.md](references/when-not-to-use.md).

## When NOT to use (brief)

- Blank-page / radical invention → use divergent methods (brainstorming, brainwriting, morphological box).
- Bottleneck is missing facts → research first, then return with a defined subject.
- Subject is vague ("our customer service") → narrow to 3–5 sentences first.

## Process

1. **Define the subject — reuse before you ask.** In 3–5 sentences: what the baseline is, its steps/touchpoints, who is involved, what works and what doesn't, and hard constraints. See [references/facilitation.md](references/facilitation.md).

2. **Choose operators.** Run all 7 sequentially (beginners, 30–45 min) or select the 3–4 most relevant to your goal (experienced teams, deeper ideas). See [references/operators.md](references/operators.md) and [scripts/scamper.py](scripts/scamper.py).

3. **Generate variations.** Per operator, brainwrite silently (2–3 ideas each, no discussion), then share. Use the operator's trigger questions; no judging or clustering yet.

4. **Cluster and enrich.** Group ideas thematically; elaborate the most promising clusters into 1–2 sentence concepts with a concrete mechanism — not just a label.

5. **Evaluate and choose.** Dot-vote or use a 2×2 (innovation potential × feasibility). Keep a portfolio of 2–3 concepts — not just the safest — each with one next experiment or probe. See [references/examples.md](references/examples.md) for a worked service example.

6. **Decide next.** Harden with `premortem`, test with users, or stretch further with `lateral-thinking` / `analogical-thinking`. Confirm with the user before running the next method.

## Rules

- One operator at a time — separate generation from evaluation.
- Subject must be concrete before you start — 3–5 sentences, not a topic label.
- Quantity with depth: quality beats racing through all 7 superficially; prefer 3–4 deep over 7 shallow.
- Keep variety honest: rewordings don't count; each idea should change the mechanism or constraint.
- Visible uncertainty: label facts, assumptions, and speculation.
- Honest provenance: attribute the checklist to Osborn/Eberle; call adaptations adaptations. See [references/provenance.md](references/provenance.md).
