# Backcasting

Start from the future you want and walk backward until you reach something you can do this quarter. The future anchors the search; the backward walk finds the route.

The failure mode it prevents is a long-term ambition with no credible path from today's actions.

## When to use

- Long-term ambition is clear but the route is not.
- Forward planning keeps producing incremental steps that do not reach the ambition.
- You need milestones that connect today to the desired future.

If unsure this is the right method, start with [thinking-router](../thinking-router/SKILL.md) — or see [references/when-not-to-use.md](references/when-not-to-use.md).

## When NOT to use (brief)

- The future is uncertain on which world will occur → `scenario-planning`.
- You need ripple effects of one change → `futures-wheel`.
- You need to explore many possible futures → `what-if`.

## Process

1. **Intake — reuse before you ask.** State the ambition, horizon, stakeholders, and why it matters. Mark fact / assumption / speculation. See [references/future.md](references/future.md).

2. **Define the desired future.** Make it observable: who experiences what, how you would know it is true. Keep it to 2–3 sentences without hedging. See [references/future.md](references/future.md) and [scripts/backcast.py](scripts/backcast.py).

3. **Step backward in time.** From the future, ask "what must be true the period before?" Repeat in 1–2 year (or quarterly) increments until you reach today. Record each step as a milestone with its enabling condition. See [references/steps.md](references/steps.md).

4. **Bridge to today.** For the last 2–3 steps, translate milestones into quarterly actions with owners and leading signals. Keep actions small enough to probe.

5. **Stress-test.** For each step ask: what would make this fail, and what alternative path recovers? Keep at least one bypass per critical milestone.

6. **Choose the first 1–2 moves.** Select the nearest actions with highest information value; define success signal and decision rule. See [references/examples.md](references/examples.md).

## Rules

- Define the future observably before stepping backward — vague futures produce vague paths.
- Step backward one period at a time; long jumps hide missing milestones.
- Every milestone must have an enabling condition and a signal that it is met.
- Label fact / assumption / speculation.
- Honest provenance: call adaptations adaptations. See [references/provenance.md](references/provenance.md).
