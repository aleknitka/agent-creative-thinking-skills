# Provenance — Attribution, Origin, and Evidence

## Named lineage

- **C-K Design Theory** — formulated by **Armand Hatchuel and Benoît Weil** (2002 onward), formalizing design reasoning as joint expansion of Concept space (C, undecidable propositions) and Knowledge space (K, verifiable facts), and the four operators C→C, C→K, K→C, K→K.
- Influences on the adaptation include Hatchuel & Weil's distinction between restrictive and expansive partitions, and the notion of design generativity as growth of the design square.
- OpenAlex references captured in the extended-methods sweep include Hatchuel/Weil's C-K corpus (`W2049599278`, `W4298440840`) and neighbours on design generativity (`W2897247950`).

## Adaptations in this skill

- The initial-concept template, the design-square record, the round structure (one expansive partition per round), and the K-pocket / learning-agenda workflow are practical adaptations for AI-assisted use.
- Call adaptations adaptations when attributing externally.

## Evidence and limits

- C-K is a design-theoretic framework, not an empirically validated intervention for all contexts. It is presented here as a disciplined heuristic for expanding the thinkable and tracking what must be learned — not as a validated decision or ideation method with effect sizes.
- Tag evidence quality (fact vs. assumption vs. speculation) and owner/date for learning agenda items.
