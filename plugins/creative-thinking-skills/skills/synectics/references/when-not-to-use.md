# When NOT to Use Synectics — and What to Use Instead

## Quick triage

| Your situation | Better fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| You need a quick one-off stimulus | `forced-connections` | One stimulus, one forced property |
| The problem needs mechanism transfer now | `analogical-thinking` | Source-driven transfer with limits |
| Situation is messy with no shared picture | `rich-picture-catwoe` | Visual sensemaking before analogy |
| Near commitment and need to harden | `premortem` | Synectics excursions before convergence |
| You have a well-scoped baseline to vary | `scamper`, `morphological-analysis` | Systematic variation, not excursion |
| You need stakeholder tradeoffs broadly | `stakeholder-perspectives` | Social mapping without analogy |

## Don't confuse

- **Synectics vs. Forced Connections:** forced connections is one stimulus with forced properties; synectics is four analogies plus an excursion and a forced return. Use synectics when one stimulus is too shallow to carry a mechanism.
- **Synectics vs. Analogical Thinking:** analogical thinking maps a known source's mechanism onto the target; synectics generates the source via excursion and personal/symbolic/fantasy estrangement.

## Handoff triggers

When Synectics isn't the right entry point, triage with `thinking-router` (*more ideas / broader perspectives*).

Synectics hands off to:

| Next | Trigger |
|---|---|
| `analogical-thinking` | Excursion surfaced a far domain worth mapping rigorously |
| `constraint-driven-ideation` | An excursion constraint proved keepable |
| `premortem` | You selected a springboard and need to harden |

## Anti-patterns

- Skipping the excursion — departure and return must be separate pages.
- Personal analogy without embodiment — "I am …" language is required.
- Symbolic analogy as slogan — compressed paradox required, not harmony.
