# Springboards — From Forced Fit to Candidates

## What a springboard is

A springboard is a partial, mechanism-flavored possibility that arises from forcing the excursion world back onto the original problem. It is not a finished solution — it is a launch point.

## Forced fit to springboard

- State the forced fit: "In the excursion world, X governs Y by means of M — in our problem, X → …"
- Turn it into a springboard: "What if we … by means of M?"

Record springboards without judging; generate 4–6 before selecting.

## Harvest

Keep 2–3 springboards with a testable mechanism. Each must point to a smallest probe — the test that would reveal whether the mechanism transfers. Tag ownership and date.
