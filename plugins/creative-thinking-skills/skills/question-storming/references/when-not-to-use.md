# When not to use

Question storming is a problem-finding method. It is the right first move when
the frame is doubtful, and the wrong move when the frame is sound or when the
missing thing is not a question.

## Do not use when

- **You need an answer now.** If the decision is hours away and the question
  is settled enough to act, storming adds delay without changing the decision.
  Record the open questions and proceed.
- **The missing input is a fact.** Questions do not substitute for a query, a
  measurement, or a user interview. If you know exactly what evidence would
  settle the matter, go get it.
- **The decision is locked.** If scope, budget, or policy cannot change, a
  storm produces frustration, not leverage. Document assumptions and monitor.
- **The work is correctness-bound.** Proofs, safety sequences, compliance
  audits, and incident response need vertical/critical thinking and domain
  procedure, not reframing.
- **You already have a sound problem and need options.** Once the frame is
  trustworthy, generate solutions with `lateral-thinking`, `scamper`, or
  `morphological-analysis`. Storming again becomes procrastination.
- **The group is in conflict about facts.** Resolve the factual dispute first;
  otherwise questions become ammunition.

## Adjacent methods

| Situation | Better method | Why |
|---|---|---|
| You need to surface and test inherited beliefs | `challenge-assumptions` | It inventories specific beliefs and probes their truth; question storming only reveals that beliefs exist |
| The frame is at the wrong level — too narrow or too abstract | `abstraction-laddering` | It deliberately moves up ("why does this matter?") and down ("how could this happen?") rather than generating breadth |
| You need new directions once the problem is framed | `lateral-thinking`, `scamper` | They produce alternatives; storming produces the question that alternatives must answer |
| You need to map a solution space | `morphological-analysis` | It enumerates dimensions and combinations; storming does not structure options |
| You need to stress-test a chosen plan | `premortem` | It assumes failure and works backward; storming still precedes commitment |
| You need many perspectives on one topic | `six-thinking-hats` | It sequences emotional, critical, and creative modes; storming is single-mode interrogation |
| You are unsure which method applies | `thinking-router` | It triages by the kind of block you have |

## Question storming vs. challenge-assumptions

They overlap at the boundary, so choose by starting material:

- If you can already name the beliefs in play — "we assume users want email,"
  "we assume approval must be manual" — use **challenge-assumptions**. It
  gives each belief a test and a reframe.
- If the brief is too vague to name beliefs, or nobody has articulated the
  frame, use **question-storming** first. Its reframing questions surface the
  candidates; challenge-assumptions then tests them.

In sequence: storm → cluster → rank → hand the load-bearing belief to
challenge-assumptions. Do not do both fully; that duplicates work and delays
the probe.

## Question storming vs. abstraction-laddering

- Use **abstraction-laddering** when you have a concrete intervention and need
  to ask "why does this matter?" until you reach a goal, or "how could this
  happen?" until you reach a mechanism.
- Use **question-storming** when you need breadth across definitions,
  stakeholders, constraints, and evidence, not a single deliberate vertical
  move.

If the storm reveals that everyone is arguing at different levels, stop and
ladder explicitly. If the ladder reveals an unexamined frame, storm around it.

## Red flags that you are using the wrong method

- The storm keeps producing the same three questions in different words →
  likely a missing fact or a locked decision, not a framing problem.
- Every question is about implementation detail → the frame was accepted
  before the storm; revisit the reframing category or use
  challenge-assumptions.
- The session ends with a solution and no revised question → evaluation
  leaked into generation; rerun the generation pass or switch to an ideation
  method.
- The requester rejects every reframing question without evidence → the
  method cannot operate; surface the authority issue and stop.
