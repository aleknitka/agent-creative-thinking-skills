# Facilitation — Blue-Hat Moves

Blue manages process so hats stay parallel. Anyone may call Blue, but Blue decides.

## Opening (Blue)

- State the **focus question** in one sentence.
- Name the **real goal** and what a good outcome looks like.
- Choose a **sequence** and **time-boxes**; announce them.
- Confirm appetite for the mode split ("We'll hold critique until Black").

Example opening: "Focus: should we ship the new onboarding this sprint? Goal: decide with risks visible. Sequence: White → Red → Yellow → Black → Green → Blue, 30 min total. Green is quantity-before-quality; no judging until Black."

## During — keeping one hat at a time

| Signal | Blue move |
|---|---|
| Someone critiques during Green | "That's Black — we'll capture it in the Black slot. Green stays generative." |
| Discussion drifts to interpretation during White | "That's Yellow/Black — White stays to what we know vs. what we'd need to verify." |
| Feelings are justified at length | "Red is brief and unjustified by design — one sentence per person." |
| Time-box expires | "Time on Yellow — extend 2 min or move to Black? Call it." |
| New information surfaces late | "New White item — capture it; decide if it changes Yellow/Black." |
| Energy drops in Green | "Pull a provocation or use the random-entry picker to restart." |
| Disagreement hardens | "Parallel check: which hat are we disagreeing from? If it's Yellow vs. Black, both are valid — we'll synthesize tension at close." |

## Closing (Blue) — synthesis template

```
White: known [bullets], gaps [bullets]
Red: reactions [bullets, subjective]
Yellow: benefits + conditions [pairs]
Black: risks + checks needed [pairs]
Green: alternatives generated [list]
Tensions: Yellow↔Black [what the tradeoff really is]
Decision: [go / iterate / park] + next step + owner + date
Dissent: [what a reasonable Black-hat objection would still be]
```

- Name tensions explicitly; don't smooth them over.
- End with one **next experiment or check**, not a vague "continue."

## Signals that Blue should intervene

- Two hats are active in the same paragraph.
- One voice dominates — Blue calls for round-robin by hat.
- Red or Black is performing rather than reporting.
- Green is self-censoring — re-emphasize quantity before quality.

## Adapting for solo or human-AI use

- With AI, both parties wear the called hat — the AI does not stay neutral or summarize prematurely.
- Solo: time-box hats even when alone; write per-hat outputs separately before synthesizing.
- For short windows, run a single focused hat (e.g., "Black only on this launch plan") and note which perspectives were intentionally skipped.
