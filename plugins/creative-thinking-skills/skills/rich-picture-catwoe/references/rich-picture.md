# Rich Picture — What to Draw and How

## What to capture on one page

- **Actors:** people and groups with stakes or influence.
- **Structures:** organization, hierarchy, boundaries.
- **Processes and flows:** what moves (information, decisions, resources, patients) and where it stalls.
- **Tensions and conflicts:** use crossed swords, jagged lines, or annotations where worldviews clash.
- **Concerns and worries:** thought bubbles for what each actor would say they fear.

Keep the picture free-form — icons, arrows, and words are all allowed. Avoid formal notation; the point is to make the mess shareable before imposing a model.

## How to draw

- Start in the center with the situation of concern; add actors outward.
- Use arrows for flows, not causality — causality comes later (CLD).
- Mark where measurement exists vs. where intuition is used.

## Quality check

The picture should reveal at least one tension that the CATWOE later makes explicit as a worldview clash. If it shows only a process, add actors and concerns.
