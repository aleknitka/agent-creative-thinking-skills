# Provenance — Attribution, Origin, and Evidence

## Named lineage

- **Rich Picture and CATWOE** — developed within **Soft Systems Methodology (SSM)** by **Peter Checkland** and colleagues (emergence 1970s, *Systems Thinking, Systems Practice*, 1981; Checkland & Poulter, *Learning for Action*, 2006). CATWOE operationalizes worldviews into root definitions. The IfM/Cambridge framing of soft vs. hard systems and the Humane Design CATWOE guide are synthesis aids.

## Adaptations in this skill

- The one-page sketch checklist, root-definition sentence template, and debate procedure are practical adaptations for workshop and AI-assisted use.
- Call adaptations adaptations when attributing externally.

## Evidence and limits

- Controlled evaluations of Rich Picture and CATWOE as standalone protocols are limited and context-dependent; case literature reports that SSM aids sensemaking for messy, multi-stakeholder situations when facilitated well. This skill is presented as a disciplined heuristic for making worldview explicit, not as an empirically validated intervention.
- Tag evidence quality (observed picture elements vs. interpretation) and probe results as fact/assumption/speculation.
