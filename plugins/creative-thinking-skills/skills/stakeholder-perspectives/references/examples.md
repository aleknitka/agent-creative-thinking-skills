# Worked Example — Who Bears Onboarding Cost

## Groups
Solo users, invited teams, success/CS, engineering, new hires (overlooked).

## Tensions
- Faster solo onboarding (solo) vs. personalized depth (teams) — speed vs. relevance.
- Invite-carried intent (engineering simplicity) vs. invite capture burden (inviter effort).

## Opportunities
- Segmented entry: solo gets short checklist, invited skips tour; instrumentation signals which tension is real.
