# Props and Space — Low-Fidelity Setup

## Space

Mark floors with tape for boundaries, queues, and thresholds. Place furniture for counters, doors, and waiting areas. Keep the footprint at real scale — miniatures hide reach and crowding.

## Props

Use cheap, suggestive objects: paper for artifacts, cups for controls, chairs for barriers. Label props with their meaning; do not build realistic models.

Required kit: tape, paper, markers, sticky notes, one timer, one camera.

## Facilitation

One facilitator watches time and prompts role swaps; they do not enact. Keep prompts to "continue" and "swap now" — interpretation waits.

## Workspace mapping

Lanes in the shared workspace (`workspace/index.html`) can map to space zones or time segments; use the six-hats lane mapping for role-based capture. Props remain physical.
