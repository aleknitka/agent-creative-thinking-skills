# Worked Example — Clinic Check-In Walkthrough

Scene: community clinic check-in for a routine visit. Goal: patient completes check-in without staff rescue.

## Roles and variants

- Roles: new patient, returning patient, front-desk staff, clinician.
- Nominal: on-time, correct documents.
- Stressed: 10 minutes late, Spanish-preferred, no smartphone.

## Props and space

Tape marks entrance, queue, desk, waiting area. Paper forms, a bell for "system beep," chairs as partition.

## Friction observed

- **Threshold:** late patient hesitates at entrance — no signage for late arrival.
- **Handoff:** staff must re-enter data already on paper; patient waits with no queue signal.
- **Recovery:** confused patient has no visible help cue; rescue depends on staff noticing.

## Clusters and probes

- Threshold → add visible late-arrival cue and queuing signal (probe: one sign, measure rescue rate).
- Handoff → carry data from paper to system without re-entry (probe: one form field pre-fill from paper scan).
- Recovery → make help request physical (raised card) rather than verbal (probe: card uptake vs. verbal requests).

Each probe is owned and dated; no prototype is built before the walkthrough.
