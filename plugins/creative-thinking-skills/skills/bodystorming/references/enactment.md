# Enactment — Roles, Scenarios, and Walkthrough

## Define the scene

For the moment to enact, state:

- **Before:** what the actor brings (intent, knowledge, tools).
- **During:** what the interaction asks the body to do (reach, wait, choose, hand off).
- **After:** what success or failure looks like observably.

List 2–4 roles (user, staff, bystander, system) and the goal each carries.

## Scenario variants

- **Nominal:** expected path, expected pace.
- **Stressed:** rushed, low-access, or information-poor actor.
- **Edge:** confused or non-standard actor (language, device, ability).

Run at least nominal plus one variant; stressed paths surface waiting and handoff gaps fastest.

## Walkthrough structure

1. Narrate the intent aloud, then enact silently at 1× speed.
2. Swap roles and repeat.
3. Replay the peak-friction segment at varied pace (0.5× and 2×) to check timing.

Keep enactment before debrief — do not interpret during the walk.

## Recording

Video or time-stamped notes with actor + location + observed friction. Mark which variant and which role.
