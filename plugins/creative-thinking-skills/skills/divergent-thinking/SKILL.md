# Divergent Thinking

Produce breadth first, judgment second. The failure mode this skill prevents is anchoring on the first plausible idea and polishing it.

## Process

1. **Restate the problem** in one sentence, plus the real goal behind it.
2. **Generate at least 10 options** without evaluating any. Include at least two that feel wrong or absurd.
3. **Force variety.** Apply 2-3 lenses from [references/lenses.md](references/lenses.md) (invert, constrain, borrow from another field, etc.) to escape the obvious cluster.
4. **Cluster** the options into themes and note which themes are unexplored.
5. **Converge.** Pick the top 2-3 using criteria stated up front, and say why the rest lost.

## Rules

- Do not critique during generation.
- Quantity before quality; aim for distinct ideas, not rewordings.
- Show the user the spread, not just the winner.
