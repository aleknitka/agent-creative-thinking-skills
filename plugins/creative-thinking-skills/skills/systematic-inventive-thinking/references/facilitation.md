# Facilitation — Timing, Discipline, and Solo/AI Mode

## Inside-the-box discipline

State the closed world up front: the listed components and immediate environment are the only allowed material. Adding a new technology, channel, or budget line is not permitted during generation — only during enrichment, and only the minimal feasible fix.

Name the list visibly so the group can police it. A component smuggled in later is a restart, not a fix.

## Timing

- **Solo or pair (20–30 min):** 5 min intake, 3–5 min per operator (one operator only for a short pass), 5 min evaluation.
- **Workshop (45–60 min):** 10 min intake + closed-world agreement, 8 min per operator (rotate operators across tables), 10 min enrichment and shortlist.
- **Inside a larger session:** use SIT as one divergence pass inside `double-diamond` (Develop) or after `question-storming`.

## Solo / AI mode

- Run one operator at a time in writing; do not generate all five virtual products at once. The AI mirrors you on the chosen operator without importing new resource types.
- Keep generation and evaluation separate; only the selected 2–3 candidates go to enrichment.
- Record each virtual product verbatim before enrichment so the infeasibility is visible.

## Artifacts

One virtual product per run, its enrichment, and a 2–3 candidate shortlist with probes. Discard variants without a qualitative change or a new benefit.
