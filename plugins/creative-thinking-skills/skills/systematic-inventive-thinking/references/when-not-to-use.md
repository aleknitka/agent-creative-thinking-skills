# When NOT to Use Systematic Inventive Thinking — and What to Use Instead

## Quick triage

| Your situation | Better fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| No concrete system is defined | Scope it or use `question-storming` | SIT needs a closed world to transform |
| You need breadth before structure | `divergent-thinking` or `brainwriting` | Quantity-first divergence before disciplined mutation |
| A recurring technical contradiction blocks progress | `triz-contradictions` | TRIZ names the tradeoff and applies separation / principles |
| You want broad, open-ended variation on a baseline | `scamper` | Seven broad operators; SIT is five narrow, formal ones |
| The constraint is actually negotiable | `challenge-assumptions` | Reframing may beat closed-world discipline |
| The work is about future possibilities broadly | `scenario-planning`, `futures-wheel` | SIT is anchored to what already exists |

## Don't confuse

- **SIT vs. SCAMPER:** SCAMPER's seven prompts are heuristics for variation; SIT's five operators are formal, closed-world transforms that keep resource types fixed. SIT is narrower and more testable; SCAMPER is broader and more generative. If you can add a new resource freely, you are not doing SIT.
- **SIT vs. TRIZ:** TRIZ analyzes contradictions and applies separation / 40 principles; SIT mutates the existing system directly inside the closed world. Use SIT when the architecture is fixed; use TRIZ when the tradeoff itself is the problem.

## Handoff triggers

When SIT isn't the right entry point, triage with `thinking-router` (*better question / more ideas / stronger options*).

SIT hands off to:

| Next | Trigger |
|---|---|
| `premortem` | You kept 2–3 candidates and need to harden before commitment |
| `scamper` | You want broader variation on the chosen virtual product after SIT's discipline |
| `triz-contradictions` | The virtual product exposes a recurring tradeoff to break |

## Anti-patterns

- Adding a new kind of resource and calling it enrichment — that breaks the closed world.
- Keeping many variants without a qualitative change; the tension of infeasibility was not used.
- Applying two operators at once and producing a hybrid that cannot be traced to one transform.
