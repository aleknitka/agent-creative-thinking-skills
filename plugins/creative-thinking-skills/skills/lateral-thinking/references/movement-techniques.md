# Movement Techniques — Turning a Po into Usable Ideas

Movement is de Bono's **active** operation for getting from a provocation to a practical direction. It is not "suspend judgment and hope" — you deliberately mine the Po for value.

Apply **2–3 moves per Po**; pick the ones that yield the most. Steps 2–3 of the skill produce ≥5 Pos, then you move the 2–3 most intriguing ones.

## The 5 moves

### 1. Extract a principle — what principle could make this Po sensible?

> "If this Po were true, what principle would have to be at work?"

- **Prompt:** Name the principle in one sentence, then restate it without the Po's absurdity.
- **Example:** `Po, onboarding has no tutorial` → principle: *competence is acquired through doing, not instruction* → direction: embed learning inside the first real task.

### 2. Focus on the difference — what is different vs. current reality?

> List what changes in the Po world; one of those differences may be valuable on its own.

- **Prompt:** "In the Po world X is different. Which difference is interesting even in isolation?"
- **Example:** `Po, the factory is downstream of itself` → difference: the polluter drinks its own waste → principle: *make the actor bear the consequence* → law: intake downstream of outfall.

### 3. Moment-to-moment — trace consequences step by step

> Walk forward from the Po as if it were true: what happens next, then next?

- **Prompt:** Write 3–4 moments: Moment 1 → Moment 2 → Moment 3 → so what?
- **Example:** `Po, traffic polices itself` → Moment 1: drivers get instant feedback → Moment 2: social pressure replaces enforcement → Moment 3: norms shift → direction: ambient feedback displays, peer-visible scores.
- This is the same structure as a light futures-wheel trace.

### 4. Positive aspects — what is intriguing even if the whole Po is nonsense?

> The whole idea may be junk; a fragment may not be.

- **Prompt:** "Even if the Po fails, what one aspect would you keep?"
- **Example:** `Po, the telephone rings once a year` → positive aspect: *calls become rare and highly valued* → direction: batch / digested notifications instead of constant pings.

### 5. Special circumstances — when/where would this Po actually be an advantage?

> Find the niche where the absurdity is a fit, then generalize inward.

- **Prompt:** "In what context is this Po's weakness actually a strength?"
- **Example:** `Po, the price is 1000×` → special circumstance: a luxury / signal market → principle: *price as filter, not just revenue* → direction: tier that deliberately excludes low-intent users.

## Worked trace — one Po end-to-end (onboarding)

**Po (escape + wishful):** `Po, onboarding has no interface — new users succeed before they start.`

| Move | Output |
|---|---|
| **Extract principle** | Success is prepared elsewhere; the entry point is pre-configured. |
| **Difference** | No first-run UI vs. current guided tour → difference worth keeping: zero UI. |
| **Moment-to-moment** | Invite arrives pre-provisioned → first open shows their real work, not a demo → learning happens by doing → tour becomes unnecessary. |
| **Positive aspect** | "Succeed before they start" → value: instant relevance. |
| **Special circumstances** | Works where context is known in advance (invite, import, template). |
| **Direction** | **Frame:** "Pre-configured entry." **Mechanism:** invitation/import carries intent so the product opens on real work. **Probe:** one import-template that skips the tour entirely. |

## Quality check

- Did you move **before** judging?
- Does each moved Po yield a distinct **mechanism or assumption challenged** (not just phrasing)?
- Can you state the direction without reusing the Po's absurd wording?
