# Abstraction — Players, Resources, Board, Loop, Win/Loss

## Intake checklist (3–5 sentences)

- Whose problem and what "good" looks like.
- Hard constraints (cannot change) and soft wants (nice to have).
- Who hurts if you fail — not who complains loudest.
- Out of scope — what you deliberately exclude to keep it playable.

## Cast (2–4 players)

Use *roles in the system*, not org chart titles. Ask: who acts, who waits, who pays, who fixes. Cut any player whose action never changes the board.

## Scarce resources (2–3)

What is truly fought over and depletable: attention, intent, capacity, trust, budget, slots. Name quantity or limit per turn — "high trust" is not a resource.

## Board (one)

Pick one topology: **Queue** (FIFO with jumps), **Track** (progress toward win/stall), **Map** (places with distance/cost), **Market** (supply/demand price). The board is where the loop lives.

## Loop (one feedback)

Write one sentence: "More X → more Y → more X" (reinforcing) or "More X → less X" (balancing) + where delay hides. Mark delay with `||`.

## Win / Loss

Both must be reachable. Win: collective or player. Loss: bust, stall, or commons collapse. If you cannot lose, you haven't modeled risk.
