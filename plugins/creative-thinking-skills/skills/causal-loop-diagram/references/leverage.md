# Leverage Points (Meadows 12)

Ordered from low to high leverage:

12. Numbers (targets, subsidies) — low
11. Buffers (slack capacity)
10. Stock-and-flow structures
9. Delays
8. Balancing loops
7. Reinforcing loops
6. Information flows
5. Rules (incentives, constraints)
4. Self-organization (ability to add/change structure)
3. Goals of the system
2. Paradigms (shared beliefs about how it works)
1. Transcending paradigms — high

## Using the list

Score each possible intervention against this hierarchy, not against effort. A low-effort fix at level 12 rarely moves a system stuck at level 2.

Record leverage as the reason for the probe's placement, not as an afterthought.

## Probe link

Leverage without a probe is a wish. For the top 1–2 points, define the smallest change, its signal, and the owner.
