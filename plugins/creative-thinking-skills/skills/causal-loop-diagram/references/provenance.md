# Provenance — Attribution, Origin, and Evidence

## Named lineage

- **Causal Loop Diagrams (CLDs)** — developed in **System Dynamics** (Jay Forrester, MIT, 1950s–60s) and elaborated in **Systems Thinking** (Senge, Sterman, Meadows). Archetypes and the 12 leverage points are attributed to **Donella Meadows** and the systems archetype literature. Rich Picture and CATWOE neighbours come from **Soft Systems Methodology (Checkland)** and precede CLD in the discovery sequence.

## Adaptations in this skill

- The intake boundary template, delay-marking rule, and probe linkage are practical adaptations for workshop and AI-assisted use.
- Call adaptations adaptations when attributing externally.

## Evidence and limits

- Controlled evaluations of CLDs as standalone protocols are limited and context-dependent; case literature reports that CLDs aid shared understanding of feedback-driven persistence when facilitated. This skill is presented as a disciplined heuristic for surfacing loops and leverage, not as an empirically validated intervention.
- Tag evidence quality (observed loop vs. hypothesized) and probe results as fact/assumption/speculation.
